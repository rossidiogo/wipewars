# UI restyle: modern dark fantasy

Date: 2026-10-11
Branch: `ui/restyle` (on top of `ui/refactor`)
Status: implemented; direction chosen by the user, result not yet reviewed by them

## Intent

Make the UI look modern while keeping the game's identity. Chosen direction: keep the dark
palette, gold accents and the Cinzel title font; drop the Path of Exile-style chrome (ornate
panel corners, stacked black/bronze borders, bevelled gradient buttons, serif body text).

## How

The whole game, legacy screens and Preact screens alike, shares the class names in
`css/style.css`, so the restyle is a stylesheet change only. No markup, JS or text changed,
which keeps translation and behaviour untouched.

- **Tokens** at the top of `style.css`: a five-step surface scale (`--well` to `--surface-3`),
  two hairline borders (`--line`, `--line-2`), text and accent colours, radii (`--r-s/m/l/pill`)
  and two shadows. New rules use tokens; leftover hardcoded browns in untouched rules were
  mapped to the nearest token.
- **Type**: Inter (system sans fallback) for everything read, Cinzel for screen titles,
  section headings, hero and chest names, stage numbers and the Battle button. Buttons and
  tabs are no longer forced to uppercase.
- **Components**: panels are flat rounded cards with one hairline border; buttons are flat
  with a 42px minimum height (34px small); primary actions are a crimson fill; tabs are a
  segmented control with a gold active segment; pills, chips, progress bars and tiles are
  rounded; the nav and headers are flat bars.
- **Not restyled on purpose**: the battle field, unit HUD bars, hit effects, the pull-reveal
  animation and cutscenes. They are pixel-art scenes, not app chrome.

## Fixes made along the way

- The title card's `.tpanel` rule also hit the bag's item panel (same class), pinning the
  empty-bag message to the bottom of the screen. The title rule is now `#tPanel`.
- The recruit reveal's `.pname` rule enlarged the player name on home; home now sets its own.
- Home currency pills are compact so all four fit on one line at 360px.

## Verification

Smoke tests (every screen, EN and PT, a played stage, mail flows) pass. The screenshot
baseline was regenerated on the new look and is stable across repeats; every 390px screenshot
plus home and battle were inspected by eye.

## Follow-ups

- `--cTL/--cTR/--cBL/--cBR` (panel corner ornaments) are still generated by
  `build/legacy-plugin.ts` but no longer used.
- Colours set inline by legacy JS (item tier names, set colours) are unchanged.
- Web fonts are stubbed in tests, so screenshots show the fallback fonts, not Inter/Cinzel.
